- D-013 [OWNER] 2026-11-22 — Workers are non-fungible individuals. - D-014 [OWNER] 2026-11-22 — Skills improve through use. Source: "Skills improve through use" (session startup-idle setup)
- D-015 [OWNER] 2026-11-22 — The Founder is just a free coder, with no special abilities. Source: "founder is just a free coder" (session startup-idle setup)
- D-016 [OWNER] 2026-11-22 — No click-to-earn; supersedes the manual "Write code"/"Pitch" actions from D-009. Source: "remove clicking" (session startup-idle setup)
